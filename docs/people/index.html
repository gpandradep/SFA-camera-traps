People

The students and collaborators behind the SFA Camera Trap Project

The SFA Camera Trap Project is built by students and collaborators who contribute at different stages of the research process — from camera deployment and image classification to data analysis, scientific posters, and independent research.

Project Wildlife Levels

These levels recognize growing experience with the camera-trap workflow. They are a way to visualize students progression through the project.

Squirrel Getting started and learning the project workflow.
Raccoon Regular contributor expanding skills and independence.
White-tailed Deer Experienced contributor taking on research or training responsibilities.
Bobcat Core team member with broad experience across multiple project components.
