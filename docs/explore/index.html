Interpreting the explorer

The 30-day bait experiment uses the first 30 days after deployment at each station and is the appropriate period for questions focused on attractant treatments. The full survey uses all available sampling effort and records.

Events / 100 trap-nights standardizes independent records by sampling effort. Observed species richness includes only categories identified as wildlife species in the project species metadata.
