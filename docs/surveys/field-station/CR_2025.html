Cherokee Ridge — 2025

Field Station – Bait Project

See the general Field Station – Bait Project methods.

Survey overview

Cherokee Ridge was surveyed in 2025 using CamPark T40 cameras. The ground survey included 13 camera-trap stations and 26 cameras, deployed from May 15, 2025 through November 21, 2025.

Treatments were:

  • Perfume: Calvin Klein Obsession
  • Fish-based bait: Sardines
  • Control: Unbaited control

Survey-specific design

Cherokee Ridge differed from the general Field Station design in two important ways:

  1. Ground stations used paired cameras rather than a single camera per station.
  2. A separate canopy-camera component was deployed outside the ground bait experiment.

The ground sampling represented in the current camera-operation data is summarized below.

Sampling effort

Sampling effort is reported for the standardized 30-day bait experiment and for the full survey period.

For the 30-day bait experiment, stations accumulated an average of 29.8 active trap-nights, with a total effort of 388.0 station trap-nights.

Across the full survey, stations accumulated an average of 89.6 active trap-nights, with a total effort of 1,165.0 station trap-nights.

Note

Because Cherokee Ridge used paired ground cameras, the number of physical cameras is larger than the number of sampling stations. A station is considered active when at least one camera in the pair is operating.

Canopy component

The separate canopy component consisted of 6 stations and 6 cameras, totaling 391.0 active trap-nights.

These cameras were not part of the ground bait-treatment experiment and should be treated separately unless a specific research question requires otherwise.

Study site

Cherokee Ridge is a privately owned property in East Texas.
